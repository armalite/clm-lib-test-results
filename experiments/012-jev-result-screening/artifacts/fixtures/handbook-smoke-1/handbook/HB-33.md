# HB-33 · Onboarding checklist
version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-01-01

§1 First week
First week: general guidance for all employees. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here.

§2 Accounts and access
Accounts and access: general guidance for all employees. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule.

§3 Mandatory trainings
Mandatory trainings: general guidance for all employees. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible.
