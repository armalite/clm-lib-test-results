# HB-01 · Travel policy: meals
version: 3 · status: current · effective: 2026-05-01 · supersedes: HB-28

§1 Scope
This policy sets the daily meal allowance for all employees. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim.

§2 Standard daily meal allowance
The daily meal allowance during business travel is 56 EUR per day. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted.

§3 Additions
For a day with an approved client dinner, 38 EUR per day is added to the standard daily meal allowance. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section.

§4 Approval and receipts
Claims follow the expense submission process. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible.

§5 Exceptions
Group exceptions to the daily meal allowance are published in HB-32. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation.
