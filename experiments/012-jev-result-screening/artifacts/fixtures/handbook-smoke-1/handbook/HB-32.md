# HB-32 · Memo: daily meal allowance exceptions
version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-03-01

§1 Purpose
This memo lists group exceptions to the daily meal allowance in HB-01. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation.

§2 Exception for client-site days in London or Paris
For client-site days in London or Paris, the daily meal allowance is 77 EUR per day instead of the standard amount. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation.

§3 Carve-out
The exception above does not apply to interns; they follow the standard daily meal allowance in HB-01 §2. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim.

§4 Review
This memo is reviewed together with the main policy. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim.
