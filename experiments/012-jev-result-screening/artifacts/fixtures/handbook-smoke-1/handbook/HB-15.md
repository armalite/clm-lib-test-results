# HB-15 · Related budgets: mobile phones
version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-06-01

§1 Scope
This document covers budgets related to, but separate from, the monthly mobile allowance. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location.

§2 Central budget
Conference-call services are billed centrally; each team's licence costs 53 EUR per month. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims.

§3 Contacts
The finance team answers questions about these budgets. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location.
