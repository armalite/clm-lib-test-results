# HB-21 · Office access and visitors
version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-01-01

§1 Badges
Badges: general guidance for all employees. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule.

§2 Visitor registration
Visitor registration: general guidance for all employees. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted.

§3 Overnight guests
Overnight guests: general guidance for all employees. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim.
