# HB-31 · Memo: monthly mobile allowance exceptions
version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-06-01

§1 Purpose
This memo lists group exceptions to the monthly mobile allowance in HB-13. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section.

§2 Exception for field engineers
For field engineers, the monthly mobile allowance is 65 EUR per month instead of the standard amount. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim.

§3 Carve-out
The exception above does not apply to field engineers on a company-paid phone contract; they follow the standard monthly mobile allowance in HB-13 §2. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim.

§4 Review
This memo is reviewed together with the main policy. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted.
