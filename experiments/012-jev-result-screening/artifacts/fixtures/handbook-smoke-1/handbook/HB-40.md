# HB-40 · Travel policy: lodging
version: 3 · status: current · effective: 2026-03-01 · supersedes: HB-26

§1 Scope
This policy sets the nightly lodging limit for all employees. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible.

§2 Standard nightly lodging limit
The nightly lodging limit for business travel is 155 EUR per night, including taxes and city fees. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location.

§3 Additions
For a stay that includes a Saturday night, 25 EUR per night is added to the standard nightly lodging limit. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim.

§4 Approval and receipts
Claims follow the expense submission process. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible.

§5 Exceptions
Group exceptions to the nightly lodging limit are published in HB-11. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim.
