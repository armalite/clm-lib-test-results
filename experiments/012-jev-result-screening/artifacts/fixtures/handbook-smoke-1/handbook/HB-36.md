# HB-36 · Information security basics
version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-01-01

§1 Passwords
Passwords: general guidance for all employees. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner.

§2 Laptops on the road
Laptops on the road: general guidance for all employees. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred.

§3 Reporting incidents
Reporting incidents: general guidance for all employees. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule.
