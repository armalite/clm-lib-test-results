# HB-29 · Related budgets: lodging
version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-06-01

§1 Scope
This document covers budgets related to, but separate from, the nightly lodging limit. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner.

§2 Central budget
Lodging for speakers at company events is booked centrally by the events team; the event budget caps each speaker room at 210 EUR per night. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department.

§3 Contacts
The finance team answers questions about these budgets. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department.
