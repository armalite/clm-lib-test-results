# HB-12 · Code of conduct
version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-01-01

§1 Respect at work
Respect at work: general guidance for all employees. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner.

§2 Conflicts of interest
Conflicts of interest: general guidance for all employees. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible.

§3 Gifts and hospitality
Gifts and hospitality: general guidance for all employees. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header.
