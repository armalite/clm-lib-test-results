# HB-38 · Memo: home-office equipment allowance exceptions
version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-07-01

§1 Purpose
This memo lists group exceptions to the home-office equipment allowance in HB-34. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section.

§2 Exception for employees with a medical accessibility plan
For employees with a medical accessibility plan, the home-office equipment allowance is 750 EUR instead of the standard amount. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim.

§3 Carve-out
The exception above does not apply to part-time employees working fewer than 20 hours a week; they follow the standard home-office equipment allowance in HB-34 §2. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment.

§4 Review
This memo is reviewed together with the main policy. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule.
