# HB-20 · Related budgets: meals
version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-03-01

§1 Scope
This document covers budgets related to, but separate from, the daily meal allowance. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred.

§2 Central budget
Team lunches during internal workshops are paid from the workshop budget, which allows 80 EUR per participant per workshop. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment.

§3 Contacts
The finance team answers questions about these budgets. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment.
