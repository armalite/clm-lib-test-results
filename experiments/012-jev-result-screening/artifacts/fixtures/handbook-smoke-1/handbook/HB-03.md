# HB-03 · Remote work: home-office equipment
version: 2 · status: superseded · effective: 2025-07-01 to the day before 2026-04-01 · superseded by: HB-34

§1 Scope
This version set the home-office equipment allowance for all employees. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment.

§2 Standard home-office equipment allowance
Employees may claim a one-off home-office equipment allowance of 300 EUR. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment.

§3 Additions
For employees who work remotely four or more days a week, 150 EUR was added to the standard home-office equipment allowance. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment.

§4 Version status
This version applied from 2025-07-01 until the day before 2026-04-01. From 2026-04-01 it is superseded by HB-34; claims for earlier dates keep this version's amounts.
