# HB-30 · Glossary of handbook terms
version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-01-01

§1 Budget owner
Budget owner: general guidance for all employees. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment.

§2 Business travel
Business travel: general guidance for all employees. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section.

§3 Claim
Claim: general guidance for all employees. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred.

§4 Policy owner
Policy owner: general guidance for all employees. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim.
