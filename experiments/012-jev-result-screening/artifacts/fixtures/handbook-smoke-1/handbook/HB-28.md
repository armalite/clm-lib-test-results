# HB-28 · Travel policy: meals
version: 2 · status: superseded · effective: 2025-03-01 to the day before 2026-05-01 · superseded by: HB-01

§1 Scope
This version set the daily meal allowance for all employees. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible.

§2 Standard daily meal allowance
The daily meal allowance during business travel was 49 EUR per day. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims.

§3 Additions
For a day with an approved client dinner, 38 EUR per day was added to the standard daily meal allowance. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment.

§4 Version status
This version applied from 2025-03-01 until the day before 2026-05-01. From 2026-05-01 it is superseded by HB-01; claims for earlier dates keep this version's amounts.
