# HB-11 · Memo: nightly lodging limit exceptions
version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-06-01

§1 Purpose
This memo lists group exceptions to the nightly lodging limit in HB-40. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department.

§2 Exception for business trips to Zurich, Geneva or Oslo
For business trips to Zurich, Geneva or Oslo, the nightly lodging limit is 250 EUR per night instead of the standard amount. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header.

§3 Carve-out
The exception above does not apply to contractors; they follow the standard nightly lodging limit in HB-40 §2. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner.

§4 Review
This memo is reviewed together with the main policy. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim.
