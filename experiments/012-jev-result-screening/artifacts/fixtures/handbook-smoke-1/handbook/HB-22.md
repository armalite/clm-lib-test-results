# HB-22 · HR policy: relocation support
version: 2 · status: superseded · effective: 2025-08-01 to the day before 2026-06-01 · superseded by: HB-08

§1 Scope
This version set the relocation lump sum for all employees. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header.

§2 Standard relocation lump sum
Employees who relocate at the company's request receive a relocation lump sum of 3750 EUR. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims.

§3 Additions
For employees relocating with dependent children, 750 EUR was added to the standard relocation lump sum. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment.

§4 Version status
This version applied from 2025-08-01 until the day before 2026-06-01. From 2026-06-01 it is superseded by HB-08; claims for earlier dates keep this version's amounts.
