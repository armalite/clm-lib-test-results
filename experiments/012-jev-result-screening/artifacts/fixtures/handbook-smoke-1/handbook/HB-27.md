# HB-27 · Related budgets: relocation support
version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-08-01

§1 Scope
This document covers budgets related to, but separate from, the relocation lump sum. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section.

§2 Central budget
Temporary housing near a new office is arranged by facilities for up to 3500 EUR per month during the first month. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header.

§3 Contacts
The finance team answers questions about these budgets. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header.
