# HB-16 · Travel policy: private car use
version: 3 · status: current · effective: 2026-06-01 · supersedes: HB-35

§1 Scope
This policy sets the mileage rate for all employees. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department.

§2 Standard mileage rate
Business kilometres driven with a private car are reimbursed at 32 cents per km. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here.

§3 Additions
For trips towing a trailer, 4 cents per km is added to the standard mileage rate. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule.

§4 Approval and receipts
Claims follow the expense submission process. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible.

§5 Exceptions
Group exceptions to the mileage rate are published in HB-17. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section.
