# HB-05 · Related budgets: approvals
version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-06-01

§1 Scope
This document covers budgets related to, but separate from, the purchase approval threshold. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible.

§2 Central budget
Petty-cash purchases are limited to 4000 EUR per receipt and need no approval. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment.

§3 Contacts
The finance team answers questions about these budgets. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment.
