# HB-17 · Memo: mileage rate exceptions
version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-04-01

§1 Purpose
This memo lists group exceptions to the mileage rate in HB-16. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location.

§2 Exception for electric vehicles
For electric vehicles, the mileage rate is 40 cents per km instead of the standard amount. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule.

§3 Carve-out
The exception above does not apply to electric rental cars; they follow the standard mileage rate in HB-16 §2. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule.

§4 Review
This memo is reviewed together with the main policy. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted.
