# HB-34 · Remote work: home-office equipment
version: 3 · status: current · effective: 2026-04-01 · supersedes: HB-03

§1 Scope
This policy sets the home-office equipment allowance for all employees. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment.

§2 Standard home-office equipment allowance
Employees may claim a one-off home-office equipment allowance of 500 EUR. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment.

§3 Additions
For employees who work remotely four or more days a week, 150 EUR is added to the standard home-office equipment allowance. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner.

§4 Approval and receipts
Claims follow the expense submission process. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation.

§5 Exceptions
Group exceptions to the home-office equipment allowance are published in HB-38. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred.
