# HB-39 · Memo: relocation lump sum exceptions
version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-08-01

§1 Purpose
This memo lists group exceptions to the relocation lump sum in HB-08. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim.

§2 Exception for relocations to another country
For relocations to another country, the relocation lump sum is 7000 EUR instead of the standard amount. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location.

§3 Carve-out
The exception above does not apply to relocations within the first six months of employment; they follow the standard relocation lump sum in HB-08 §2. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here.

§4 Review
This memo is reviewed together with the main policy. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred.
