# HB-37 · Communication policy: mobile phones
version: 2 · status: superseded · effective: 2025-06-01 to the day before 2026-05-01 · superseded by: HB-13

§1 Scope
This version set the monthly mobile allowance for all employees. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department.

§2 Standard monthly mobile allowance
Employees who use a private phone for work receive a mobile allowance of 30 EUR per month. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted.

§3 Additions
For employees on international rotation, 22 EUR per month was added to the standard monthly mobile allowance. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location.

§4 Version status
This version applied from 2025-06-01 until the day before 2026-05-01. From 2026-05-01 it is superseded by HB-13; claims for earlier dates keep this version's amounts.
