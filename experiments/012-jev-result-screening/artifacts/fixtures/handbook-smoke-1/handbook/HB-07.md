# HB-07 · Learning policy: training budget
version: 3 · status: current · effective: 2026-03-01 · supersedes: HB-06

§1 Scope
This policy sets the annual training budget for all employees. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header.

§2 Standard annual training budget
Each employee has an annual training budget of 900 EUR per calendar year. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header.

§3 Additions
For employees who hold a team-lead role, 500 EUR per year is added to the standard annual training budget. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred.

§4 Approval and receipts
Claims follow the expense submission process. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule.

§5 Exceptions
Group exceptions to the annual training budget are published in HB-09. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule.
