# HB-18 · Memo: purchase approval threshold exceptions
version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-06-01

§1 Purpose
This memo lists group exceptions to the purchase approval threshold in HB-10. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule.

§2 Exception for the facilities department
For the facilities department, the purchase approval threshold is 8000 EUR instead of the standard amount. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred.

§3 Carve-out
The exception above does not apply to IT hardware bought by the facilities department; they follow the standard purchase approval threshold in HB-10 §2. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here.

§4 Review
This memo is reviewed together with the main policy. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred.
