# HB-26 · Travel policy: lodging
version: 2 · status: superseded · effective: 2025-06-01 to the day before 2026-03-01 · superseded by: HB-40

§1 Scope
This version set the nightly lodging limit for all employees. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted.

§2 Standard nightly lodging limit
The nightly lodging limit for business travel was 200 EUR per night, including taxes and city fees. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section.

§3 Additions
For a stay that includes a Saturday night, 25 EUR per night was added to the standard nightly lodging limit. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred.

§4 Version status
This version applied from 2025-06-01 until the day before 2026-03-01. From 2026-03-01 it is superseded by HB-40; claims for earlier dates keep this version's amounts.
