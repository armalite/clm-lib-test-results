# HB-02 · Procurement policy: approvals
version: 2 · status: superseded · effective: 2025-06-01 to the day before 2026-05-01 · superseded by: HB-10

§1 Scope
This version set the purchase approval threshold for all employees. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner.

§2 Standard purchase approval threshold
Purchases up to 2750 EUR may be approved by the budget owner alone; larger purchases need a second approval. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted.

§3 Additions
For purchases from suppliers with a framework contract, 2000 EUR was added to the standard purchase approval threshold. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section.

§4 Version status
This version applied from 2025-06-01 until the day before 2026-05-01. From 2026-05-01 it is superseded by HB-10; claims for earlier dates keep this version's amounts.
