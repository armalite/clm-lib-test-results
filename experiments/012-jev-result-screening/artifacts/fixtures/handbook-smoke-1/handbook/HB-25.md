# HB-25 · Expense submission process
version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-01-01

§1 Submitting a claim
Submitting a claim: general guidance for all employees. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner.

§2 Approval routing
Approval routing: general guidance for all employees. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department.

§3 Payment dates
Payment dates: general guidance for all employees. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department.
