# HB-23 · Public holidays and office closures
version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-01-01

§1 Public holidays
Public holidays: general guidance for all employees. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation.

§2 Year-end closure
Year-end closure: general guidance for all employees. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department.

§3 Bridge days
Bridge days: general guidance for all employees. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location.
