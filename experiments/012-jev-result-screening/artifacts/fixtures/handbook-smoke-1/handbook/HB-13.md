# HB-13 · Communication policy: mobile phones
version: 3 · status: current · effective: 2026-05-01 · supersedes: HB-37

§1 Scope
This policy sets the monthly mobile allowance for all employees. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department.

§2 Standard monthly mobile allowance
Employees who use a private phone for work receive a mobile allowance of 28 EUR per month. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim.

§3 Additions
For employees on international rotation, 22 EUR per month is added to the standard monthly mobile allowance. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner.

§4 Approval and receipts
Claims follow the expense submission process. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted.

§5 Exceptions
Group exceptions to the monthly mobile allowance are published in HB-31. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner.
