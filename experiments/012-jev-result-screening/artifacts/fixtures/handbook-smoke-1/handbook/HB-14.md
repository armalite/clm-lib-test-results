# HB-14 · Related budgets: home-office equipment
version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-07-01

§1 Scope
This document covers budgets related to, but separate from, the home-office equipment allowance. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted.

§2 Central budget
Office desks are refurbished on a five-year cycle; each refurbishment is budgeted at 575 EUR per desk. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred.

§3 Contacts
The finance team answers questions about these budgets. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here.
