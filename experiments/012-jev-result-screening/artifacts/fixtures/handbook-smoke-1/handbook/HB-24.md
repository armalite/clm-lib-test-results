# HB-24 · Related budgets: private car use
version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-04-01

§1 Scope
This document covers budgets related to, but separate from, the mileage rate. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section.

§2 Central budget
Company pool cars are charged to projects at an internal rate of 37 cents per km to cover fuel and maintenance. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header.

§3 Contacts
The finance team answers questions about these budgets. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section.
