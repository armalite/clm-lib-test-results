# HB-09 · Memo: annual training budget exceptions
version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-07-01

§1 Purpose
This memo lists group exceptions to the annual training budget in HB-07. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim.

§2 Exception for employees in the security team
For employees in the security team, the annual training budget is 2100 EUR per year instead of the standard amount. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section.

§3 Carve-out
The exception above does not apply to employees still in their probation period; they follow the standard annual training budget in HB-07 §2. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim.

§4 Review
This memo is reviewed together with the main policy. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted.
