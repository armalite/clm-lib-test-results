# HB-10 · Procurement policy: approvals
version: 3 · status: current · effective: 2026-05-01 · supersedes: HB-02

§1 Scope
This policy sets the purchase approval threshold for all employees. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred.

§2 Standard purchase approval threshold
Purchases up to 4000 EUR may be approved by the budget owner alone; larger purchases need a second approval. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred.

§3 Additions
For purchases from suppliers with a framework contract, 2000 EUR is added to the standard purchase approval threshold. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here.

§4 Approval and receipts
Claims follow the expense submission process. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule.

§5 Exceptions
Group exceptions to the purchase approval threshold are published in HB-18. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header.
