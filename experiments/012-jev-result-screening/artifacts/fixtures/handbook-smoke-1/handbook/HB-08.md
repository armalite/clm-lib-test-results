# HB-08 · HR policy: relocation support
version: 3 · status: current · effective: 2026-06-01 · supersedes: HB-22

§1 Scope
This policy sets the relocation lump sum for all employees. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header.

§2 Standard relocation lump sum
Employees who relocate at the company's request receive a relocation lump sum of 3000 EUR. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims.

§3 Additions
For employees relocating with dependent children, 750 EUR is added to the standard relocation lump sum. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred.

§4 Approval and receipts
Claims follow the expense submission process. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible.

§5 Exceptions
Group exceptions to the relocation lump sum are published in HB-39. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department.
