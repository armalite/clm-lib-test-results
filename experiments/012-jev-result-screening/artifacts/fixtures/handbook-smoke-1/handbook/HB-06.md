# HB-06 · Learning policy: training budget
version: 2 · status: superseded · effective: 2025-07-01 to the day before 2026-03-01 · superseded by: HB-07

§1 Scope
This version set the annual training budget for all employees. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner.

§2 Standard annual training budget
Each employee has an annual training budget of 1350 EUR per calendar year. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location.

§3 Additions
For employees who hold a team-lead role, 500 EUR per year was added to the standard annual training budget. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation.

§4 Version status
This version applied from 2025-07-01 until the day before 2026-03-01. From 2026-03-01 it is superseded by HB-07; claims for earlier dates keep this version's amounts.
