# HB-35 · Travel policy: private car use
version: 2 · status: superseded · effective: 2025-04-01 to the day before 2026-06-01 · superseded by: HB-16

§1 Scope
This version set the mileage rate for all employees. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim.

§2 Standard mileage rate
Business kilometres driven with a private car are reimbursed at 28 cents per km. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment.

§3 Additions
For trips towing a trailer, 4 cents per km was added to the standard mileage rate. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted.

§4 Version status
This version applied from 2025-04-01 until the day before 2026-06-01. From 2026-06-01 it is superseded by HB-16; claims for earlier dates keep this version's amounts.
