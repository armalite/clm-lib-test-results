# HB-19 · Related budgets: training budget
version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-07-01

§1 Scope
This document covers budgets related to, but separate from, the annual training budget. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department.

§2 Central budget
Conference attendance is funded by the department conference pool, which reserves 1350 EUR per department each year. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim.

§3 Contacts
The finance team answers questions about these budgets. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred.
