# HB-17 · Travel policy: meals
version: 2 · status: superseded · effective: 2025-09-01 to the day before 2026-05-01 · superseded by: HB-27

§1 Scope
This version set the daily meal allowance for all employees. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred.

§2 Standard daily meal allowance
The daily meal allowance during business travel was 47 EUR per day. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims.

§3 Additions
For a day with an approved client dinner, 30 EUR per day was added to the standard daily meal allowance. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department.

§4 Version status
This version applied from 2025-09-01 until the day before 2026-05-01. From 2026-05-01 it is superseded by HB-27; claims for earlier dates keep this version's amounts.
