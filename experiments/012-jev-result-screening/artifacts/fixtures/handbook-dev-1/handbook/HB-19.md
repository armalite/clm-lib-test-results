# HB-19 · Related budgets: approvals
version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-04-01

§1 Scope
This document covers budgets related to, but separate from, the purchase approval threshold. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner.

§2 Central budget
Petty-cash purchases are limited to 4250 EUR per receipt and need no approval. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section.

§3 Contacts
The finance team answers questions about these budgets. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule.
