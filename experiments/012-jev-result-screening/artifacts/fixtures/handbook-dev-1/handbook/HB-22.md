# HB-22 · Travel policy: lodging
version: 3 · status: current · effective: 2026-02-01 · supersedes: HB-13

§1 Scope
This policy sets the nightly lodging limit for all employees. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department.

§2 Standard nightly lodging limit
The nightly lodging limit for business travel is 150 EUR per night, including taxes and city fees. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section.

§3 Additions
For a stay that includes a Saturday night, 25 EUR per night is added to the standard nightly lodging limit. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner.

§4 Approval and receipts
Claims follow the expense submission process. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred.

§5 Exceptions
Group exceptions to the nightly lodging limit are published in HB-03. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here.
