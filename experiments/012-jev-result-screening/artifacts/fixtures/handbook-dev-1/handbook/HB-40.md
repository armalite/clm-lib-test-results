# HB-40 · Related budgets: relocation support
version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-01-01

§1 Scope
This document covers budgets related to, but separate from, the relocation lump sum. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted.

§2 Central budget
Temporary housing near a new office is arranged by facilities for up to 3500 EUR per month during the first month. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible.

§3 Contacts
The finance team answers questions about these budgets. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department.
