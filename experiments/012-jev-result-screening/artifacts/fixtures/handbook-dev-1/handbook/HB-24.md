# HB-24 · Related budgets: home-office equipment
version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-07-01

§1 Scope
This document covers budgets related to, but separate from, the home-office equipment allowance. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location.

§2 Central budget
Office desks are refurbished on a five-year cycle; each refurbishment is budgeted at 525 EUR per desk. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location.

§3 Contacts
The finance team answers questions about these budgets. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim.
