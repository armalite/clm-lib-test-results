# HB-29 · Travel policy: private car use
version: 3 · status: current · effective: 2026-06-01 · supersedes: HB-38

§1 Scope
This policy sets the mileage rate for all employees. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule.

§2 Standard mileage rate
Business kilometres driven with a private car are reimbursed at 34 cents per km. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner.

§3 Additions
For trips towing a trailer, 4 cents per km is added to the standard mileage rate. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims.

§4 Approval and receipts
Claims follow the expense submission process. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule.

§5 Exceptions
Group exceptions to the mileage rate are published in HB-39. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim.
