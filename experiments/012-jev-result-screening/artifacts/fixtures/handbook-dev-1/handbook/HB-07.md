# HB-07 · HR policy: relocation support
version: 2 · status: superseded · effective: 2025-01-01 to the day before 2026-06-01 · superseded by: HB-35

§1 Scope
This version set the relocation lump sum for all employees. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule.

§2 Standard relocation lump sum
Employees who relocate at the company's request receive a relocation lump sum of 3750 EUR. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment.

§3 Additions
For employees relocating with dependent children, 1000 EUR was added to the standard relocation lump sum. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment.

§4 Version status
This version applied from 2025-01-01 until the day before 2026-06-01. From 2026-06-01 it is superseded by HB-35; claims for earlier dates keep this version's amounts.
