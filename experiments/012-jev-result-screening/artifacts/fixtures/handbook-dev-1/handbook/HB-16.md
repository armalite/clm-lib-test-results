# HB-16 · Procurement policy: approvals
version: 2 · status: superseded · effective: 2025-04-01 to the day before 2026-02-01 · superseded by: HB-08

§1 Scope
This version set the purchase approval threshold for all employees. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim.

§2 Standard purchase approval threshold
Purchases up to 3750 EUR may be approved by the budget owner alone; larger purchases need a second approval. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment.

§3 Additions
For purchases from suppliers with a framework contract, 2000 EUR was added to the standard purchase approval threshold. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible.

§4 Version status
This version applied from 2025-04-01 until the day before 2026-02-01. From 2026-02-01 it is superseded by HB-08; claims for earlier dates keep this version's amounts.
