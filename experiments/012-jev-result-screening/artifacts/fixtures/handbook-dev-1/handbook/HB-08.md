# HB-08 · Procurement policy: approvals
version: 3 · status: current · effective: 2026-02-01 · supersedes: HB-16

§1 Scope
This policy sets the purchase approval threshold for all employees. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims.

§2 Standard purchase approval threshold
Purchases up to 2500 EUR may be approved by the budget owner alone; larger purchases need a second approval. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible.

§3 Additions
For purchases from suppliers with a framework contract, 2000 EUR is added to the standard purchase approval threshold. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner.

§4 Approval and receipts
Claims follow the expense submission process. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location.

§5 Exceptions
Group exceptions to the purchase approval threshold are published in HB-01. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner.
