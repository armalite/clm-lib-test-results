# HB-20 · Related budgets: mobile phones
version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-03-01

§1 Scope
This document covers budgets related to, but separate from, the monthly mobile allowance. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims.

§2 Central budget
Conference-call services are billed centrally; each team's licence costs 47 EUR per month. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here.

§3 Contacts
The finance team answers questions about these budgets. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment.
