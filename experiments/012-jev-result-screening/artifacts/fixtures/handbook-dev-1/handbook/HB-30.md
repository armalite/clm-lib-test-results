# HB-30 · Related budgets: private car use
version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-09-01

§1 Scope
This document covers budgets related to, but separate from, the mileage rate. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims.

§2 Central budget
Company pool cars are charged to projects at an internal rate of 34 cents per km to cover fuel and maintenance. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment.

§3 Contacts
The finance team answers questions about these budgets. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment.
