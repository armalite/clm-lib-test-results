# HB-39 · Memo: mileage rate exceptions
version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-09-01

§1 Purpose
This memo lists group exceptions to the mileage rate in HB-29. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim.

§2 Exception for electric vehicles
For electric vehicles, the mileage rate is 46 cents per km instead of the standard amount. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment.

§3 Carve-out
The exception above does not apply to electric rental cars; they follow the standard mileage rate in HB-29 §2. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner.

§4 Review
This memo is reviewed together with the main policy. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner.
