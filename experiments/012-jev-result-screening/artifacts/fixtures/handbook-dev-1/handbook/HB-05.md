# HB-05 · Related budgets: training budget
version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-03-01

§1 Scope
This document covers budgets related to, but separate from, the annual training budget. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner.

§2 Central budget
Conference attendance is funded by the department conference pool, which reserves 1650 EUR per department each year. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims.

§3 Contacts
The finance team answers questions about these budgets. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred.
