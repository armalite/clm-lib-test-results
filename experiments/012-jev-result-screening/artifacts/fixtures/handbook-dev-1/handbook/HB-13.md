# HB-13 · Travel policy: lodging
version: 2 · status: superseded · effective: 2025-06-01 to the day before 2026-02-01 · superseded by: HB-22

§1 Scope
This version set the nightly lodging limit for all employees. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner.

§2 Standard nightly lodging limit
The nightly lodging limit for business travel was 210 EUR per night, including taxes and city fees. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim.

§3 Additions
For a stay that includes a Saturday night, 25 EUR per night was added to the standard nightly lodging limit. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location.

§4 Version status
This version applied from 2025-06-01 until the day before 2026-02-01. From 2026-02-01 it is superseded by HB-22; claims for earlier dates keep this version's amounts.
