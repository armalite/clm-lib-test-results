# HB-14 · Public holidays and office closures
version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-01-01

§1 Public holidays
Public holidays: general guidance for all employees. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment.

§2 Year-end closure
Year-end closure: general guidance for all employees. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment.

§3 Bridge days
Bridge days: general guidance for all employees. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted.
