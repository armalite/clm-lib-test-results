# HB-21 · Glossary of handbook terms
version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-01-01

§1 Budget owner
Budget owner: general guidance for all employees. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section.

§2 Business travel
Business travel: general guidance for all employees. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment.

§3 Claim
Claim: general guidance for all employees. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header.

§4 Policy owner
Policy owner: general guidance for all employees. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section.
