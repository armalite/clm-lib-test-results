# HB-32 · Remote work: home-office equipment
version: 2 · status: superseded · effective: 2025-07-01 to the day before 2026-03-01 · superseded by: HB-11

§1 Scope
This version set the home-office equipment allowance for all employees. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted.

§2 Standard home-office equipment allowance
Employees may claim a one-off home-office equipment allowance of 400 EUR. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible.

§3 Additions
For employees who work remotely four or more days a week, 150 EUR was added to the standard home-office equipment allowance. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department.

§4 Version status
This version applied from 2025-07-01 until the day before 2026-03-01. From 2026-03-01 it is superseded by HB-11; claims for earlier dates keep this version's amounts.
