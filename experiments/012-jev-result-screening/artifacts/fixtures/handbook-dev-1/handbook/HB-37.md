# HB-37 · Communication policy: mobile phones
version: 3 · status: current · effective: 2026-06-01 · supersedes: HB-31

§1 Scope
This policy sets the monthly mobile allowance for all employees. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim.

§2 Standard monthly mobile allowance
Employees who use a private phone for work receive a mobile allowance of 40 EUR per month. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim.

§3 Additions
For employees on international rotation, 16 EUR per month is added to the standard monthly mobile allowance. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner.

§4 Approval and receipts
Claims follow the expense submission process. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation.

§5 Exceptions
Group exceptions to the monthly mobile allowance are published in HB-25. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation.
