# HB-23 · Learning policy: training budget
version: 2 · status: superseded · effective: 2025-03-01 to the day before 2026-02-01 · superseded by: HB-18

§1 Scope
This version set the annual training budget for all employees. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header.

§2 Standard annual training budget
Each employee has an annual training budget of 1450 EUR per calendar year. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule.

§3 Additions
For employees who hold a team-lead role, 550 EUR per year was added to the standard annual training budget. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted.

§4 Version status
This version applied from 2025-03-01 until the day before 2026-02-01. From 2026-02-01 it is superseded by HB-18; claims for earlier dates keep this version's amounts.
