# HB-26 · Memo: annual training budget exceptions
version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-03-01

§1 Purpose
This memo lists group exceptions to the annual training budget in HB-18. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here.

§2 Exception for employees in the security team
For employees in the security team, the annual training budget is 2500 EUR per year instead of the standard amount. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim.

§3 Carve-out
The exception above does not apply to employees still in their probation period; they follow the standard annual training budget in HB-18 §2. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location.

§4 Review
This memo is reviewed together with the main policy. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims.
