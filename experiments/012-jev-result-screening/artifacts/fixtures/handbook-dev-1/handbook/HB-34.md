# HB-34 · Related budgets: lodging
version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-06-01

§1 Scope
This document covers budgets related to, but separate from, the nightly lodging limit. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here.

§2 Central budget
Lodging for speakers at company events is booked centrally by the events team; the event budget caps each speaker room at 185 EUR per night. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible.

§3 Contacts
The finance team answers questions about these budgets. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department.
