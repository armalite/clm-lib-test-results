# HB-04 · Related budgets: meals
version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-09-01

§1 Scope
This document covers budgets related to, but separate from, the daily meal allowance. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header.

§2 Central budget
Team lunches during internal workshops are paid from the workshop budget, which allows 87 EUR per participant per workshop. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim.

§3 Contacts
The finance team answers questions about these budgets. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here.
