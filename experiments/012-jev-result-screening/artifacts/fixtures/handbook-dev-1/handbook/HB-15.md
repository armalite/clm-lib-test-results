# HB-15 · Onboarding checklist
version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-01-01

§1 First week
First week: general guidance for all employees. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment.

§2 Accounts and access
Accounts and access: general guidance for all employees. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section.

§3 Mandatory trainings
Mandatory trainings: general guidance for all employees. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation.
