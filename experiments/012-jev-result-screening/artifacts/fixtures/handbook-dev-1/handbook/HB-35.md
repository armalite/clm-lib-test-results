# HB-35 · HR policy: relocation support
version: 3 · status: current · effective: 2026-06-01 · supersedes: HB-07

§1 Scope
This policy sets the relocation lump sum for all employees. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner.

§2 Standard relocation lump sum
Employees who relocate at the company's request receive a relocation lump sum of 3500 EUR. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here.

§3 Additions
For employees relocating with dependent children, 1000 EUR is added to the standard relocation lump sum. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section.

§4 Approval and receipts
Claims follow the expense submission process. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location.

§5 Exceptions
Group exceptions to the relocation lump sum are published in HB-10. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here.
