# HB-36 · Information security basics
version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-01-01

§1 Passwords
Passwords: general guidance for all employees. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment.

§2 Laptops on the road
Laptops on the road: general guidance for all employees. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner.

§3 Reporting incidents
Reporting incidents: general guidance for all employees. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims.
