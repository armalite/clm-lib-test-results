# HB-11 · Remote work: home-office equipment
version: 3 · status: current · effective: 2026-03-01 · supersedes: HB-32

§1 Scope
This policy sets the home-office equipment allowance for all employees. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule.

§2 Standard home-office equipment allowance
Employees may claim a one-off home-office equipment allowance of 500 EUR. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header.

§3 Additions
For employees who work remotely four or more days a week, 150 EUR is added to the standard home-office equipment allowance. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment.

§4 Approval and receipts
Claims follow the expense submission process. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header.

§5 Exceptions
Group exceptions to the home-office equipment allowance are published in HB-09. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred.
