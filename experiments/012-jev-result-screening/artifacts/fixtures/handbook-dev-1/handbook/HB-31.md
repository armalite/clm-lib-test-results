# HB-31 · Communication policy: mobile phones
version: 2 · status: superseded · effective: 2025-03-01 to the day before 2026-06-01 · superseded by: HB-37

§1 Scope
This version set the monthly mobile allowance for all employees. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule.

§2 Standard monthly mobile allowance
Employees who use a private phone for work receive a mobile allowance of 36 EUR per month. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section.

§3 Additions
For employees on international rotation, 16 EUR per month was added to the standard monthly mobile allowance. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner.

§4 Version status
This version applied from 2025-03-01 until the day before 2026-06-01. From 2026-06-01 it is superseded by HB-37; claims for earlier dates keep this version's amounts.
