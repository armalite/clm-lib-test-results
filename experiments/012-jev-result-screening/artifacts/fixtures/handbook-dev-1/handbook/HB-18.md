# HB-18 · Learning policy: training budget
version: 3 · status: current · effective: 2026-02-01 · supersedes: HB-23

§1 Scope
This policy sets the annual training budget for all employees. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment.

§2 Standard annual training budget
Each employee has an annual training budget of 1150 EUR per calendar year. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim.

§3 Additions
For employees who hold a team-lead role, 550 EUR per year is added to the standard annual training budget. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner.

§4 Approval and receipts
Claims follow the expense submission process. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department.

§5 Exceptions
Group exceptions to the annual training budget are published in HB-26. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner.
