# HB-28 · Code of conduct
version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-01-01

§1 Respect at work
Respect at work: general guidance for all employees. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred.

§2 Conflicts of interest
Conflicts of interest: general guidance for all employees. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred.

§3 Gifts and hospitality
Gifts and hospitality: general guidance for all employees. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred.
