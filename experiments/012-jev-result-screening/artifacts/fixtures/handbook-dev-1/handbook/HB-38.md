# HB-38 · Travel policy: private car use
version: 2 · status: superseded · effective: 2025-09-01 to the day before 2026-06-01 · superseded by: HB-29

§1 Scope
This version set the mileage rate for all employees. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner.

§2 Standard mileage rate
Business kilometres driven with a private car are reimbursed at 36 cents per km. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible.

§3 Additions
For trips towing a trailer, 4 cents per km was added to the standard mileage rate. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred.

§4 Version status
This version applied from 2025-09-01 until the day before 2026-06-01. From 2026-06-01 it is superseded by HB-29; claims for earlier dates keep this version's amounts.
