# HB-33 · Memo: daily meal allowance exceptions
version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-09-01

§1 Purpose
This memo lists group exceptions to the daily meal allowance in HB-27. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim.

§2 Exception for client-site days in London or Paris
For client-site days in London or Paris, the daily meal allowance is 84 EUR per day instead of the standard amount. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted.

§3 Carve-out
The exception above does not apply to interns; they follow the standard daily meal allowance in HB-27 §2. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here.

§4 Review
This memo is reviewed together with the main policy. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment.
