# HB-01 · Memo: purchase approval threshold exceptions
version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-04-01

§1 Purpose
This memo lists group exceptions to the purchase approval threshold in HB-08. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section.

§2 Exception for the facilities department
For the facilities department, the purchase approval threshold is 8500 EUR instead of the standard amount. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment.

§3 Carve-out
The exception above does not apply to IT hardware bought by the facilities department; they follow the standard purchase approval threshold in HB-08 §2. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred.

§4 Review
This memo is reviewed together with the main policy. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment.
