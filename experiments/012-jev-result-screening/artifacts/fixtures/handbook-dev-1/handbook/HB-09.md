# HB-09 · Memo: home-office equipment allowance exceptions
version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-07-01

§1 Purpose
This memo lists group exceptions to the home-office equipment allowance in HB-11. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department.

§2 Exception for employees with a medical accessibility plan
For employees with a medical accessibility plan, the home-office equipment allowance is 750 EUR instead of the standard amount. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted.

§3 Carve-out
The exception above does not apply to part-time employees working fewer than 20 hours a week; they follow the standard home-office equipment allowance in HB-11 §2. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment.

§4 Review
This memo is reviewed together with the main policy. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section.
