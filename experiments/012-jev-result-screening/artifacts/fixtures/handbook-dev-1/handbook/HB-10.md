# HB-10 · Memo: relocation lump sum exceptions
version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-01-01

§1 Purpose
This memo lists group exceptions to the relocation lump sum in HB-35. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department.

§2 Exception for relocations to another country
For relocations to another country, the relocation lump sum is 6500 EUR instead of the standard amount. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment.

§3 Carve-out
The exception above does not apply to relocations within the first six months of employment; they follow the standard relocation lump sum in HB-35 §2. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred.

§4 Review
This memo is reviewed together with the main policy. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location.
