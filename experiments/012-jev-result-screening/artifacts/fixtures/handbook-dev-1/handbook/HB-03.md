# HB-03 · Memo: nightly lodging limit exceptions
version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-06-01

§1 Purpose
This memo lists group exceptions to the nightly lodging limit in HB-22. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner.

§2 Exception for business trips to Zurich, Geneva or Oslo
For business trips to Zurich, Geneva or Oslo, the nightly lodging limit is 270 EUR per night instead of the standard amount. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible.

§3 Carve-out
The exception above does not apply to contractors; they follow the standard nightly lodging limit in HB-22 §2. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim.

§4 Review
This memo is reviewed together with the main policy. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule.
