# HB-02 · Office access and visitors
version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-01-01

§1 Badges
Badges: general guidance for all employees. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here.

§2 Visitor registration
Visitor registration: general guidance for all employees. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible.

§3 Overnight guests
Overnight guests: general guidance for all employees. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner.
