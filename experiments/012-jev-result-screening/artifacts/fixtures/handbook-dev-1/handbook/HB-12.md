# HB-12 · Expense submission process
version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-01-01

§1 Submitting a claim
Submitting a claim: general guidance for all employees. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner.

§2 Approval routing
Approval routing: general guidance for all employees. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment.

§3 Payment dates
Payment dates: general guidance for all employees. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted.
