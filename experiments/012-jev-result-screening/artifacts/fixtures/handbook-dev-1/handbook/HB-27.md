# HB-27 · Travel policy: meals
version: 3 · status: current · effective: 2026-05-01 · supersedes: HB-17

§1 Scope
This policy sets the daily meal allowance for all employees. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment.

§2 Standard daily meal allowance
The daily meal allowance during business travel is 54 EUR per day. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment.

§3 Additions
For a day with an approved client dinner, 30 EUR per day is added to the standard daily meal allowance. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department.

§4 Approval and receipts
Claims follow the expense submission process. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department.

§5 Exceptions
Group exceptions to the daily meal allowance are published in HB-33. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim.
