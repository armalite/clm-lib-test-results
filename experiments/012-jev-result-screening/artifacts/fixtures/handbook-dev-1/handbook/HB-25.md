# HB-25 · Memo: monthly mobile allowance exceptions
version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-03-01

§1 Purpose
This memo lists group exceptions to the monthly mobile allowance in HB-37. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation.

§2 Exception for field engineers
For field engineers, the monthly mobile allowance is 66 EUR per month instead of the standard amount. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim.

§3 Carve-out
The exception above does not apply to field engineers on a company-paid phone contract; they follow the standard monthly mobile allowance in HB-37 §2. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim.

§4 Review
This memo is reviewed together with the main policy. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment.
