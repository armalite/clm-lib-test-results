# HB-15 · Procurement policy: approvals
version: 3 · status: current · effective: 2026-05-01 · supersedes: HB-11

§1 Scope
This policy sets the purchase approval threshold for all employees. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner.

§2 Standard purchase approval threshold
Purchases up to 4000 EUR may be approved by the budget owner alone; larger purchases need a second approval. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation.

§3 Additions
For purchases from suppliers with a framework contract, 1750 EUR is added to the standard purchase approval threshold. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section.

§4 Approval and receipts
Claims follow the expense submission process. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner.

§5 Exceptions
Group exceptions to the purchase approval threshold are published in HB-22. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred.
