# HB-14 · Expense submission process
version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-01-01

§1 Submitting a claim
Submitting a claim: general guidance for all employees. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule.

§2 Approval routing
Approval routing: general guidance for all employees. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted.

§3 Payment dates
Payment dates: general guidance for all employees. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim.
