# HB-34 · Communication policy: mobile phones
version: 2 · status: superseded · effective: 2025-01-01 to the day before 2026-05-01 · superseded by: HB-04

§1 Scope
This version set the monthly mobile allowance for all employees. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department.

§2 Standard monthly mobile allowance
Employees who use a private phone for work receive a mobile allowance of 38 EUR per month. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible.

§3 Additions
For employees on international rotation, 22 EUR per month was added to the standard monthly mobile allowance. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment.

§4 Version status
This version applied from 2025-01-01 until the day before 2026-05-01. From 2026-05-01 it is superseded by HB-04; claims for earlier dates keep this version's amounts.
