# HB-32 · Learning policy: training budget
version: 3 · status: current · effective: 2026-02-01 · supersedes: HB-09

§1 Scope
This policy sets the annual training budget for all employees. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim.

§2 Standard annual training budget
Each employee has an annual training budget of 1250 EUR per calendar year. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim.

§3 Additions
For employees who hold a team-lead role, 400 EUR per year is added to the standard annual training budget. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department.

§4 Approval and receipts
Claims follow the expense submission process. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule.

§5 Exceptions
Group exceptions to the annual training budget are published in HB-30. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation.
