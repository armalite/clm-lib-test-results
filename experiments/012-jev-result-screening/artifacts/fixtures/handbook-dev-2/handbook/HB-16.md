# HB-16 · Related budgets: relocation support
version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-02-01

§1 Scope
This document covers budgets related to, but separate from, the relocation lump sum. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment.

§2 Central budget
Temporary housing near a new office is arranged by facilities for up to 4750 EUR per month during the first month. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header.

§3 Contacts
The finance team answers questions about these budgets. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred.
