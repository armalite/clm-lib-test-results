# HB-10 · Memo: mileage rate exceptions
version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-06-01

§1 Purpose
This memo lists group exceptions to the mileage rate in HB-24. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted.

§2 Exception for electric vehicles
For electric vehicles, the mileage rate is 45 cents per km instead of the standard amount. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims.

§3 Carve-out
The exception above does not apply to electric rental cars; they follow the standard mileage rate in HB-24 §2. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred.

§4 Review
This memo is reviewed together with the main policy. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department.
