# HB-19 · HR policy: relocation support
version: 2 · status: superseded · effective: 2025-02-01 to the day before 2026-02-01 · superseded by: HB-23

§1 Scope
This version set the relocation lump sum for all employees. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here.

§2 Standard relocation lump sum
Employees who relocate at the company's request receive a relocation lump sum of 3000 EUR. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section.

§3 Additions
For employees relocating with dependent children, 500 EUR was added to the standard relocation lump sum. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment.

§4 Version status
This version applied from 2025-02-01 until the day before 2026-02-01. From 2026-02-01 it is superseded by HB-23; claims for earlier dates keep this version's amounts.
