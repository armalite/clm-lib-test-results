# HB-39 · Remote work: home-office equipment
version: 3 · status: current · effective: 2026-06-01 · supersedes: HB-28

§1 Scope
This policy sets the home-office equipment allowance for all employees. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment.

§2 Standard home-office equipment allowance
Employees may claim a one-off home-office equipment allowance of 375 EUR. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section.

§3 Additions
For employees who work remotely four or more days a week, 175 EUR is added to the standard home-office equipment allowance. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible.

§4 Approval and receipts
Claims follow the expense submission process. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner.

§5 Exceptions
Group exceptions to the home-office equipment allowance are published in HB-08. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted.
