# HB-33 · Travel policy: meals
version: 3 · status: current · effective: 2026-02-01 · supersedes: HB-01

§1 Scope
This policy sets the daily meal allowance for all employees. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule.

§2 Standard daily meal allowance
The daily meal allowance during business travel is 60 EUR per day. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header.

§3 Additions
For a day with an approved client dinner, 28 EUR per day is added to the standard daily meal allowance. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible.

§4 Approval and receipts
Claims follow the expense submission process. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location.

§5 Exceptions
Group exceptions to the daily meal allowance are published in HB-35. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header.
