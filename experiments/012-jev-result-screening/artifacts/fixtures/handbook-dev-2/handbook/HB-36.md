# HB-36 · Glossary of handbook terms
version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-01-01

§1 Budget owner
Budget owner: general guidance for all employees. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims.

§2 Business travel
Business travel: general guidance for all employees. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred.

§3 Claim
Claim: general guidance for all employees. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule.

§4 Policy owner
Policy owner: general guidance for all employees. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section.
