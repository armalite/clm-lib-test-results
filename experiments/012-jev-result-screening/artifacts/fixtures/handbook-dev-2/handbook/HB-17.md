# HB-17 · Office access and visitors
version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-01-01

§1 Badges
Badges: general guidance for all employees. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation.

§2 Visitor registration
Visitor registration: general guidance for all employees. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation.

§3 Overnight guests
Overnight guests: general guidance for all employees. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department.
