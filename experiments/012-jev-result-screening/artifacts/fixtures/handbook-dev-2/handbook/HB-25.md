# HB-25 · Related budgets: approvals
version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-03-01

§1 Scope
This document covers budgets related to, but separate from, the purchase approval threshold. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section.

§2 Central budget
Petty-cash purchases are limited to 4250 EUR per receipt and need no approval. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here.

§3 Contacts
The finance team answers questions about these budgets. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment.
