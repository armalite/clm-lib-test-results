# HB-40 · Code of conduct
version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-01-01

§1 Respect at work
Respect at work: general guidance for all employees. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred.

§2 Conflicts of interest
Conflicts of interest: general guidance for all employees. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner.

§3 Gifts and hospitality
Gifts and hospitality: general guidance for all employees. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation.
