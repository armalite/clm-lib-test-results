# HB-05 · Related budgets: home-office equipment
version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-03-01

§1 Scope
This document covers budgets related to, but separate from, the home-office equipment allowance. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule.

§2 Central budget
Office desks are refurbished on a five-year cycle; each refurbishment is budgeted at 650 EUR per desk. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here.

§3 Contacts
The finance team answers questions about these budgets. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section.
