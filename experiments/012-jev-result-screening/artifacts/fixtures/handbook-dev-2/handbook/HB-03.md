# HB-03 · Travel policy: private car use
version: 2 · status: superseded · effective: 2025-06-01 to the day before 2026-04-01 · superseded by: HB-24

§1 Scope
This version set the mileage rate for all employees. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims.

§2 Standard mileage rate
Business kilometres driven with a private car are reimbursed at 36 cents per km. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment.

§3 Additions
For trips towing a trailer, 8 cents per km was added to the standard mileage rate. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred.

§4 Version status
This version applied from 2025-06-01 until the day before 2026-04-01. From 2026-04-01 it is superseded by HB-24; claims for earlier dates keep this version's amounts.
