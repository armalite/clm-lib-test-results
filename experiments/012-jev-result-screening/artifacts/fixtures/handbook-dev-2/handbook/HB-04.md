# HB-04 · Communication policy: mobile phones
version: 3 · status: current · effective: 2026-05-01 · supersedes: HB-34

§1 Scope
This policy sets the monthly mobile allowance for all employees. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim.

§2 Standard monthly mobile allowance
Employees who use a private phone for work receive a mobile allowance of 32 EUR per month. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner.

§3 Additions
For employees on international rotation, 22 EUR per month is added to the standard monthly mobile allowance. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim.

§4 Approval and receipts
Claims follow the expense submission process. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment.

§5 Exceptions
Group exceptions to the monthly mobile allowance are published in HB-29. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim.
