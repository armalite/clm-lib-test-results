# HB-28 · Remote work: home-office equipment
version: 2 · status: superseded · effective: 2025-03-01 to the day before 2026-06-01 · superseded by: HB-39

§1 Scope
This version set the home-office equipment allowance for all employees. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims.

§2 Standard home-office equipment allowance
Employees may claim a one-off home-office equipment allowance of 500 EUR. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section.

§3 Additions
For employees who work remotely four or more days a week, 175 EUR was added to the standard home-office equipment allowance. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims.

§4 Version status
This version applied from 2025-03-01 until the day before 2026-06-01. From 2026-06-01 it is superseded by HB-39; claims for earlier dates keep this version's amounts.
