# HB-29 · Memo: monthly mobile allowance exceptions
version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-01-01

§1 Purpose
This memo lists group exceptions to the monthly mobile allowance in HB-04. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred.

§2 Exception for field engineers
For field engineers, the monthly mobile allowance is 66 EUR per month instead of the standard amount. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment.

§3 Carve-out
The exception above does not apply to field engineers on a company-paid phone contract; they follow the standard monthly mobile allowance in HB-04 §2. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location.

§4 Review
This memo is reviewed together with the main policy. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment.
