# HB-20 · Related budgets: mobile phones
version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-01-01

§1 Scope
This document covers budgets related to, but separate from, the monthly mobile allowance. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header.

§2 Central budget
Conference-call services are billed centrally; each team's licence costs 54 EUR per month. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location.

§3 Contacts
The finance team answers questions about these budgets. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner.
