# HB-35 · Memo: daily meal allowance exceptions
version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-01-01

§1 Purpose
This memo lists group exceptions to the daily meal allowance in HB-33. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule.

§2 Exception for client-site days in London or Paris
For client-site days in London or Paris, the daily meal allowance is 76 EUR per day instead of the standard amount. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section.

§3 Carve-out
The exception above does not apply to interns; they follow the standard daily meal allowance in HB-33 §2. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner.

§4 Review
This memo is reviewed together with the main policy. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location.
