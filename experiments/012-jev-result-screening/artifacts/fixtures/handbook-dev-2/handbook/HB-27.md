# HB-27 · Related budgets: training budget
version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-08-01

§1 Scope
This document covers budgets related to, but separate from, the annual training budget. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner.

§2 Central budget
Conference attendance is funded by the department conference pool, which reserves 1600 EUR per department each year. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment.

§3 Contacts
The finance team answers questions about these budgets. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment.
