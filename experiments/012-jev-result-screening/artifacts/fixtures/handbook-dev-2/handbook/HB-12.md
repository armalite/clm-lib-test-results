# HB-12 · Public holidays and office closures
version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-01-01

§1 Public holidays
Public holidays: general guidance for all employees. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner.

§2 Year-end closure
Year-end closure: general guidance for all employees. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here.

§3 Bridge days
Bridge days: general guidance for all employees. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment.
