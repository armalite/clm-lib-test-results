# HB-23 · HR policy: relocation support
version: 3 · status: current · effective: 2026-02-01 · supersedes: HB-19

§1 Scope
This policy sets the relocation lump sum for all employees. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred.

§2 Standard relocation lump sum
Employees who relocate at the company's request receive a relocation lump sum of 2500 EUR. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred.

§3 Additions
For employees relocating with dependent children, 500 EUR is added to the standard relocation lump sum. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment.

§4 Approval and receipts
Claims follow the expense submission process. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment.

§5 Exceptions
Group exceptions to the relocation lump sum are published in HB-18. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims.
