# HB-30 · Memo: annual training budget exceptions
version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-08-01

§1 Purpose
This memo lists group exceptions to the annual training budget in HB-32. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation.

§2 Exception for employees in the security team
For employees in the security team, the annual training budget is 2150 EUR per year instead of the standard amount. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment.

§3 Carve-out
The exception above does not apply to employees still in their probation period; they follow the standard annual training budget in HB-32 §2. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner.

§4 Review
This memo is reviewed together with the main policy. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible.
