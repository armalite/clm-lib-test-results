# HB-31 · Travel policy: lodging
version: 2 · status: superseded · effective: 2025-05-01 to the day before 2026-06-01 · superseded by: HB-21

§1 Scope
This version set the nightly lodging limit for all employees. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section.

§2 Standard nightly lodging limit
The nightly lodging limit for business travel was 180 EUR per night, including taxes and city fees. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim.

§3 Additions
For a stay that includes a Saturday night, 40 EUR per night was added to the standard nightly lodging limit. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department.

§4 Version status
This version applied from 2025-05-01 until the day before 2026-06-01. From 2026-06-01 it is superseded by HB-21; claims for earlier dates keep this version's amounts.
