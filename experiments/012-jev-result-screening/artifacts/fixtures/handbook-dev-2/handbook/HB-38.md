# HB-38 · Related budgets: private car use
version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-06-01

§1 Scope
This document covers budgets related to, but separate from, the mileage rate. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule.

§2 Central budget
Company pool cars are charged to projects at an internal rate of 36 cents per km to cover fuel and maintenance. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation.

§3 Contacts
The finance team answers questions about these budgets. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible.
