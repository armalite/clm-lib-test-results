# HB-26 · Related budgets: meals
version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-01-01

§1 Scope
This document covers budgets related to, but separate from, the daily meal allowance. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims.

§2 Central budget
Team lunches during internal workshops are paid from the workshop budget, which allows 87 EUR per participant per workshop. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule.

§3 Contacts
The finance team answers questions about these budgets. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here.
