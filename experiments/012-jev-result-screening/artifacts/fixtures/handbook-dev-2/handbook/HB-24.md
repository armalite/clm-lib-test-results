# HB-24 · Travel policy: private car use
version: 3 · status: current · effective: 2026-04-01 · supersedes: HB-03

§1 Scope
This policy sets the mileage rate for all employees. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner.

§2 Standard mileage rate
Business kilometres driven with a private car are reimbursed at 32 cents per km. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department.

§3 Additions
For trips towing a trailer, 8 cents per km is added to the standard mileage rate. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule.

§4 Approval and receipts
Claims follow the expense submission process. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred.

§5 Exceptions
Group exceptions to the mileage rate are published in HB-10. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule.
