# HB-06 · Memo: nightly lodging limit exceptions
version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-05-01

§1 Purpose
This memo lists group exceptions to the nightly lodging limit in HB-21. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location.

§2 Exception for business trips to Zurich, Geneva or Oslo
For business trips to Zurich, Geneva or Oslo, the nightly lodging limit is 240 EUR per night instead of the standard amount. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header.

§3 Carve-out
The exception above does not apply to contractors; they follow the standard nightly lodging limit in HB-21 §2. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation.

§4 Review
This memo is reviewed together with the main policy. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header.
