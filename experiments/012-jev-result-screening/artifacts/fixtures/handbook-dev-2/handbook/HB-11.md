# HB-11 · Procurement policy: approvals
version: 2 · status: superseded · effective: 2025-03-01 to the day before 2026-05-01 · superseded by: HB-15

§1 Scope
This version set the purchase approval threshold for all employees. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header.

§2 Standard purchase approval threshold
Purchases up to 2000 EUR may be approved by the budget owner alone; larger purchases need a second approval. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible.

§3 Additions
For purchases from suppliers with a framework contract, 1750 EUR was added to the standard purchase approval threshold. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location.

§4 Version status
This version applied from 2025-03-01 until the day before 2026-05-01. From 2026-05-01 it is superseded by HB-15; claims for earlier dates keep this version's amounts.
