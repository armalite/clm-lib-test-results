# HB-08 · Memo: home-office equipment allowance exceptions
version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-03-01

§1 Purpose
This memo lists group exceptions to the home-office equipment allowance in HB-39. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims.

§2 Exception for employees with a medical accessibility plan
For employees with a medical accessibility plan, the home-office equipment allowance is 675 EUR instead of the standard amount. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department.

§3 Carve-out
The exception above does not apply to part-time employees working fewer than 20 hours a week; they follow the standard home-office equipment allowance in HB-39 §2. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred.

§4 Review
This memo is reviewed together with the main policy. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims.
