# HB-09 · Learning policy: training budget
version: 2 · status: superseded · effective: 2025-08-01 to the day before 2026-02-01 · superseded by: HB-32

§1 Scope
This version set the annual training budget for all employees. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred.

§2 Standard annual training budget
Each employee has an annual training budget of 950 EUR per calendar year. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim.

§3 Additions
For employees who hold a team-lead role, 400 EUR per year was added to the standard annual training budget. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim.

§4 Version status
This version applied from 2025-08-01 until the day before 2026-02-01. From 2026-02-01 it is superseded by HB-32; claims for earlier dates keep this version's amounts.
