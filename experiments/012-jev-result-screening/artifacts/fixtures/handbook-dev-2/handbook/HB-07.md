# HB-07 · Information security basics
version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-01-01

§1 Passwords
Passwords: general guidance for all employees. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule.

§2 Laptops on the road
Laptops on the road: general guidance for all employees. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here.

§3 Reporting incidents
Reporting incidents: general guidance for all employees. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment.
