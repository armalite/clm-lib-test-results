# HB-18 · Memo: relocation lump sum exceptions
version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-02-01

§1 Purpose
This memo lists group exceptions to the relocation lump sum in HB-23. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment.

§2 Exception for relocations to another country
For relocations to another country, the relocation lump sum is 5250 EUR instead of the standard amount. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred.

§3 Carve-out
The exception above does not apply to relocations within the first six months of employment; they follow the standard relocation lump sum in HB-23 §2. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted.

§4 Review
This memo is reviewed together with the main policy. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header.
