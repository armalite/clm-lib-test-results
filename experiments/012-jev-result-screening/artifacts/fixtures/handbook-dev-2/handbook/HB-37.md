# HB-37 · Related budgets: lodging
version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-05-01

§1 Scope
This document covers budgets related to, but separate from, the nightly lodging limit. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment.

§2 Central budget
Lodging for speakers at company events is booked centrally by the events team; the event budget caps each speaker room at 195 EUR per night. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section.

§3 Contacts
The finance team answers questions about these budgets. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim.
