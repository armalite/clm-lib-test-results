# HB-01 · Travel policy: meals
version: 2 · status: superseded · effective: 2025-01-01 to the day before 2026-02-01 · superseded by: HB-33

§1 Scope
This version set the daily meal allowance for all employees. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim.

§2 Standard daily meal allowance
The daily meal allowance during business travel was 44 EUR per day. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation.

§3 Additions
For a day with an approved client dinner, 28 EUR per day was added to the standard daily meal allowance. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible.

§4 Version status
This version applied from 2025-01-01 until the day before 2026-02-01. From 2026-02-01 it is superseded by HB-33; claims for earlier dates keep this version's amounts.
