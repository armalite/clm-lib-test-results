# HB-22 · Memo: purchase approval threshold exceptions
version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-03-01

§1 Purpose
This memo lists group exceptions to the purchase approval threshold in HB-15. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred.

§2 Exception for the facilities department
For the facilities department, the purchase approval threshold is 10500 EUR instead of the standard amount. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here.

§3 Carve-out
The exception above does not apply to IT hardware bought by the facilities department; they follow the standard purchase approval threshold in HB-15 §2. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible.

§4 Review
This memo is reviewed together with the main policy. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location.
