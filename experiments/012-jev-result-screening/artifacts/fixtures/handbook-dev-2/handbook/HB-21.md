# HB-21 · Travel policy: lodging
version: 3 · status: current · effective: 2026-06-01 · supersedes: HB-31

§1 Scope
This policy sets the nightly lodging limit for all employees. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule.

§2 Standard nightly lodging limit
The nightly lodging limit for business travel is 210 EUR per night, including taxes and city fees. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location. Receipts must be attached for every item claimed; photographs of paper receipts are accepted.

§3 Additions
For a stay that includes a Saturday night, 40 EUR per night is added to the standard nightly lodging limit. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment.

§4 Approval and receipts
Claims follow the expense submission process. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule.

§5 Exceptions
Group exceptions to the nightly lodging limit are published in HB-06. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here.
