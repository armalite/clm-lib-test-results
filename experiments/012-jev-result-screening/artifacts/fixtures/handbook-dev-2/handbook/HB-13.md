# HB-13 · Onboarding checklist
version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-01-01

§1 First week
First week: general guidance for all employees. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location.

§2 Accounts and access
Accounts and access: general guidance for all employees. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment.

§3 Mandatory trainings
Mandatory trainings: general guidance for all employees. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible.
