--- HB-07 §3 | Learning policy: training budget — Additions | version: 3 · status: current · effective: 2026-03-01 · supersedes: HB-06
For employees who hold a team-lead role, 500 EUR per year is added to the standard annual training budget. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred.
--- HB-07 §2 | Learning policy: training budget — Standard annual training budget | version: 3 · status: current · effective: 2026-03-01 · supersedes: HB-06
Each employee has an annual training budget of 900 EUR per calendar year. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header.
