--- HB-03 §2 | Travel policy: private car use — Standard mileage rate | version: 2 · status: superseded · effective: 2025-06-01 to the day before 2026-04-01 · superseded by: HB-24
Business kilometres driven with a private car are reimbursed at 36 cents per km. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment.
--- HB-24 §2 | Travel policy: private car use — Standard mileage rate | version: 3 · status: current · effective: 2026-04-01 · supersedes: HB-03
Business kilometres driven with a private car are reimbursed at 32 cents per km. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department.
