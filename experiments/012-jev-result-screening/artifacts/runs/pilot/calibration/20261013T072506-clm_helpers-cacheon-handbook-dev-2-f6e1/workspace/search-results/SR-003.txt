--- HB-02 §3 | Policy FAQ — Current home-office equipment allowance | version: 7 · status: current · effective: 2026-01-15
Q: What is the current standard home-office equipment allowance? A: 375 EUR, as set in HB-39 §2 (effective 2026-06-01). Group exceptions are listed in HB-08.
--- HB-39 §2 | Remote work: home-office equipment — Standard home-office equipment allowance | version: 3 · status: current · effective: 2026-06-01 · supersedes: HB-28
Employees may claim a one-off home-office equipment allowance of 375 EUR. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section.
--- HB-28 §2 | Remote work: home-office equipment — Standard home-office equipment allowance | version: 2 · status: superseded · effective: 2025-03-01 to the day before 2026-06-01 · superseded by: HB-39
Employees may claim a one-off home-office equipment allowance of 500 EUR. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section.
--- HB-08 §1 | Memo: home-office equipment allowance exceptions — Purpose | version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-03-01
This memo lists group exceptions to the home-office equipment allowance in HB-39. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims.
--- HB-28 §1 | Remote work: home-office equipment — Scope | version: 2 · status: superseded · effective: 2025-03-01 to the day before 2026-06-01 · superseded by: HB-39
This version set the home-office equipment allowance for all employees. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims.
