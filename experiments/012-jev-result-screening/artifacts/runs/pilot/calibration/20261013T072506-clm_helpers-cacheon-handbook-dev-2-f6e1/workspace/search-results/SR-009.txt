--- HB-22 §2 | Memo: purchase approval threshold exceptions — Exception for the facilities department | version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-03-01
For the facilities department, the purchase approval threshold is 10500 EUR instead of the standard amount. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here.

--- HB-02 §6 | Policy FAQ — Current purchase approval threshold | version: 7 · status: current · effective: 2026-01-15
Q: What is the current standard purchase approval threshold? A: 4000 EUR, as set in HB-15 §2 (effective 2026-05-01). Group exceptions are listed in HB-22.
--- HB-22 §1 | Memo: purchase approval threshold exceptions — Purpose | version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-03-01
This memo lists group exceptions to the purchase approval threshold in HB-15. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment. Claims that combine several costs must list each cost on a separate line of the claim. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred.
