--- HB-18 §2 | Memo: relocation lump sum exceptions — Exception for relocations to another country | version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-02-01
For relocations to another country, the relocation lump sum is 5250 EUR instead of the standard amount. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred.
--- HB-02 §2 | Policy FAQ — Current relocation lump sum | version: 7 · status: current · effective: 2026-01-15
Q: What is the current standard relocation lump sum? A: 2500 EUR, as set in HB-23 §2 (effective 2026-02-01). Group exceptions are listed in HB-18.
--- HB-19 §2 | HR policy: relocation support — Standard relocation lump sum | version: 2 · status: superseded · effective: 2025-02-01 to the day before 2026-02-01 · superseded by: HB-23
Employees who relocate at the company's request receive a relocation lump sum of 3000 EUR. Any deviation from this section must be explained in the comment field of the claim. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section.
--- HB-23 §2 | HR policy: relocation support — Standard relocation lump sum | version: 3 · status: current · effective: 2026-02-01 · supersedes: HB-19
Employees who relocate at the company's request receive a relocation lump sum of 2500 EUR. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible. Claims are submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred.
--- HB-18 §1 | Memo: relocation lump sum exceptions — Purpose | version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-02-01
This memo lists group exceptions to the relocation lump sum in HB-23. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment.
