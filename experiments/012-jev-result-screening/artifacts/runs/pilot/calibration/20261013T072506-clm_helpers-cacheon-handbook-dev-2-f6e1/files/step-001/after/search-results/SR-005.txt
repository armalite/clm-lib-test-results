--- HB-32 §3 | Learning policy: training budget — Additions | version: 3 · status: current · effective: 2026-02-01 · supersedes: HB-09
For employees who hold a team-lead role, 400 EUR per year is added to the standard annual training budget. Costs paid with a company card are reconciled in the same tool as personal claims. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department.
--- HB-27 §1 | Related budgets: training budget — Scope | version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-08-01
This document covers budgets related to, but separate from, the annual training budget. Amounts are stated in euros; conversions use the published rate on the day of payment. Questions about this section go to the policy owner named in the document header. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner.
--- HB-30 §1 | Memo: annual training budget exceptions — Purpose | version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-08-01
This memo lists group exceptions to the annual training budget in HB-32. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule. Audit samples are drawn every quarter, and selected employees are asked for documentation.
--- HB-30 §2 | Memo: annual training budget exceptions — Exception for employees in the security team | version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-08-01
For employees in the security team, the annual training budget is 2150 EUR per year instead of the standard amount. Managers may reject claims that are not supported by the documentation described here. Where a local agreement sets stricter rules, the stricter rule applies for that location. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment.
