--- HB-35 §2 | Memo: daily meal allowance exceptions — Exception for client-site days in London or Paris | version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-01-01
For client-site days in London or Paris, the daily meal allowance is 76 EUR per day instead of the standard amount. Line managers check each claim for plausibility before it is released for payment. Repeated late or incomplete claims may be escalated to the head of the department. The policy owner may publish clarifications in the policy FAQ without changing this section.
--- HB-33 §3 | Travel policy: meals — Additions | version: 3 · status: current · effective: 2026-02-01 · supersedes: HB-01
For a day with an approved client dinner, 28 EUR per day is added to the standard daily meal allowance. This section is reviewed once a year by the finance team together with the policy owner. Supporting records are kept for seven years in line with the retention schedule. Employees should keep costs reasonable and book through the approved channels where possible.
