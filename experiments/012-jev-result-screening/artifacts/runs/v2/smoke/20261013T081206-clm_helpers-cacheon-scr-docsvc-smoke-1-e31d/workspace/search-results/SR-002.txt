--- HB-58 §2 | Memo: annual training budget exceptions — Exception for employees in the security team | version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-08-01
For employees in the security team, the annual training budget is 2700 EUR per year instead of the standard amount. The learning team reviews the annual training budget once a year and announces changes in advance. Claims for the annual training budget are filed in the expense tool under 'Learning policy: training budget'.

--- HB-46 §2 | Learning policy: training budget — Standard annual training budget | version: 3 · status: current · effective: 2026-02-01 · supersedes: HB-72
Each employee has an annual training budget of 1400 EUR per calendar year. The learning team publishes worked examples of the annual training budget on the intranet. Claims for the annual training budget are filed in the expense tool under 'Learning policy: training budget'.

For employees who hold a team-lead role, 400 EUR per year is added to the standard annual training budget.
