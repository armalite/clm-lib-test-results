--- HB-38 §2 | Communication policy: mobile phones — Standard monthly mobile allowance | version: 3 · status: current · effective: 2026-02-01 · supersedes: HB-44
Employees who use a private phone for work receive a mobile allowance of 38 EUR per month. Claims for the monthly mobile allowance are filed in the expense tool under 'Communication policy: mobile phones'. Questions about the monthly mobile allowance go to the IT, who keeps the record of decisions.
--- HB-44 §2 | Communication policy: mobile phones — Standard monthly mobile allowance | version: 2 · status: superseded · effective: 2025-09-01 to the day before 2026-02-01 · superseded by: HB-38
Employees who use a private phone for work receive a mobile allowance of 27 EUR per month. Managers confirm claims for the monthly mobile allowance before payment in the next payroll run. Questions about the monthly mobile allowance go to the IT, who keeps the record of decisions.
--- HB-08 §3 | Memo: monthly mobile allowance exceptions — Carve-out | version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-09-01
The exception above does not apply to field engineers on a company-paid phone contract; they follow the standard monthly mobile allowance in HB-38 §2. Claims for the monthly mobile allowance are filed in the expense tool under 'Communication policy: mobile phones'. Each claim for the monthly mobile allowance needs a receipt showing the date, the amount and the supplier.
--- HB-08 §1 | Memo: monthly mobile allowance exceptions — Purpose | version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-09-01
This memo lists group exceptions to the monthly mobile allowance in HB-38. The IT publishes worked examples of the monthly mobile allowance on the intranet. Each claim for the monthly mobile allowance needs a receipt showing the date, the amount and the supplier.
--- HB-44 §1 | Communication policy: mobile phones — Scope | version: 2 · status: superseded · effective: 2025-09-01 to the day before 2026-02-01 · superseded by: HB-38
This version set the monthly mobile allowance for all employees. Questions about the monthly mobile allowance go to the IT, who keeps the record of decisions. The IT publishes worked examples of the monthly mobile allowance on the intranet.
--- HB-38 §4 | Communication policy: mobile phones — Exceptions | version: 3 · status: current · effective: 2026-02-01 · supersedes: HB-44
Group exceptions to the monthly mobile allowance are published in HB-08. The IT publishes worked examples of the monthly mobile allowance on the intranet. The IT reviews the monthly mobile allowance once a year and announces changes in advance.
