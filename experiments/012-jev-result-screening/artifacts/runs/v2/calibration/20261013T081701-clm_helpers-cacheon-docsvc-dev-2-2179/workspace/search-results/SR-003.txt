--- HB-56 §3 | Travel policy: flights — Additions | version: 3 · status: current · effective: 2026-05-01 · supersedes: HB-10
For flights that require checked equipment, 50 EUR per return flight is added to the standard flight fare limit. Managers confirm claims for the flight fare limit before payment in the next payroll run. The travel office publishes worked examples of the flight fare limit on the intranet.
--- HB-10 §2 | Travel policy: flights — Standard flight fare limit | version: 2 · status: superseded · effective: 2025-03-01 to the day before 2026-05-01 · superseded by: HB-56
The flight fare limit for an economy return flight was 325 EUR. Questions about the flight fare limit go to the travel office, who keeps the record of decisions. The travel office publishes worked examples of the flight fare limit on the intranet.
--- HB-56 §2 | Travel policy: flights — Standard flight fare limit | version: 3 · status: current · effective: 2026-05-01 · supersedes: HB-10
The flight fare limit for an economy return flight is 400 EUR. Managers confirm claims for the flight fare limit before payment in the next payroll run. Claims for the flight fare limit are filed in the expense tool under 'Travel policy: flights'.
--- HB-62 §2 | Memo: flight fare limit exceptions — Exception for intercontinental flights | version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-03-01
For intercontinental flights, the flight fare limit is 800 EUR per return flight instead of the standard amount. Claims for the flight fare limit are filed in the expense tool under 'Travel policy: flights'. The travel office publishes worked examples of the flight fare limit on the intranet.
--- HB-09 §7 | Policy FAQ: travel and expenses — Current flight fare limit | version: 7 · status: current · effective: 2026-01-15
Q: What is the current standard flight fare limit? A: 400 EUR per return flight, as set in HB-56 §2 (effective 2026-05-01). Group exceptions are listed in HB-62.
--- HB-62 §1 | Memo: flight fare limit exceptions — Purpose | version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-03-01
This memo lists group exceptions to the flight fare limit in HB-56. The travel office publishes worked examples of the flight fare limit on the intranet. Each claim for the flight fare limit needs a receipt showing the date, the amount and the supplier.
