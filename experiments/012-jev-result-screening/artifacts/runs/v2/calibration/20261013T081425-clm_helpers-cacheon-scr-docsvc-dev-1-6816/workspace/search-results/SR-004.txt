--- HB-71 §2 | Memo: paid parental leave exceptions — Exception for employees who are the primary carer | version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-02-01
For employees who are the primary carer, the paid parental leave is 19 weeks instead of the standard amount. Each claim for the paid parental leave needs a receipt showing the date, the amount and the supplier. Questions about the paid parental leave go to the HR, who keeps the record of decisions.
--- HB-71 §3 | Memo: paid parental leave exceptions — Carve-out | version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-02-01
The exception above does not apply to employees in their first year of employment; they follow the standard paid parental leave in HB-06 §2. The HR reviews the paid parental leave once a year and announces changes in advance. The HR publishes worked examples of the paid parental leave on the intranet.
--- HB-06 §1 | HR policy: parental leave — Scope | version: 3 · status: current · effective: 2026-03-01 · supersedes: HB-25
This policy sets the paid parental leave for all employees. The HR reviews the paid parental leave once a year and announces changes in advance. Managers confirm claims for the paid parental leave before payment in the next payroll run.
--- HB-06 §3 | HR policy: parental leave — Additions | version: 3 · status: current · effective: 2026-03-01 · supersedes: HB-25
For parents of multiple births, 4 weeks is added to the standard paid parental leave. Questions about the paid parental leave go to the HR, who keeps the record of decisions. The HR reviews the paid parental leave once a year and announces changes in advance.
--- HB-25 §2 | HR policy: parental leave — Standard paid parental leave | version: 2 · status: superseded · effective: 2025-02-01 to the day before 2026-03-01 · superseded by: HB-06
Each parent may take 9 weeks of paid parental leave. Claims for the paid parental leave are filed in the expense tool under 'HR policy: parental leave'. Managers confirm claims for the paid parental leave before payment in the next payroll run.
