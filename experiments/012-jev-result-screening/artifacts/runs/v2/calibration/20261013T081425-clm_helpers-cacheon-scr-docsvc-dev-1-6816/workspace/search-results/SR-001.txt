--- HB-24 §2 | Memo: home-office equipment allowance exceptions — Exception for employees with a medical accessibility plan | version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-02-01
For employees with a medical accessibility plan, the home-office equipment allowance is 750 EUR instead of the standard amount. Each claim for the home-office equipment allowance needs a receipt showing the date, the amount and the supplier. Claims for the home-office equipment allowance are filed in the expense tool under 'Remote work: home-office equipment'.
--- HB-24 §3 | Memo: home-office equipment allowance exceptions — Carve-out | version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-02-01
The exception above does not apply to part-time employees working fewer than 20 hours a week; they follow the standard home-office equipment allowance in HB-62 §2. Managers confirm claims for the home-office equipment allowance before payment in the next payroll run. Claims for the home-office equipment allowance are filed in the expense tool under 'Remote work: home-office equipment'.
--- HB-18 §2 | Remote work: home-office equipment — Standard home-office equipment allowance | version: 2 · status: superseded · effective: 2025-02-01 to the day before 2026-05-01 · superseded by: HB-62
Employees may claim a one-off home-office equipment allowance of 400 EUR. Claims for the home-office equipment allowance are filed in the expense tool under 'Remote work: home-office equipment'. Each claim for the home-office equipment allowance needs a receipt showing the date, the amount and the supplier.
--- HB-62 §2 | Remote work: home-office equipment — Standard home-office equipment allowance | version: 3 · status: current · effective: 2026-05-01 · supersedes: HB-18
Employees may claim a one-off home-office equipment allowance of 375 EUR. Claims for the home-office equipment allowance are filed in the expense tool under 'Remote work: home-office equipment'. Managers confirm claims for the home-office equipment allowance before payment in the next payroll run.
--- HB-48 §1 | Related budgets: home-office equipment — Scope | version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-02-01
This document covers budgets related to, but separate from, the home-office equipment allowance. Claims for the home-office equipment allowance are filed in the expense tool under 'Remote work: home-office equipment'. Questions about the home-office equipment allowance go to the facilities, who keeps the record of decisions.
