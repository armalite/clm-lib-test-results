--- HB-47 §2 | Travel policy: taxis and ride-hailing — Standard taxi allowance | version: 2 · status: superseded · effective: 2025-02-01 to the day before 2026-03-01 · superseded by: HB-38
A single taxi or ride-hailing trip on business was reimbursed up to 45 EUR. Claims for the taxi allowance are filed in the expense tool under 'Travel policy: taxis and ride-hailing'. Questions about the taxi allowance go to the travel office, who keeps the record of decisions.
--- HB-38 §2 | Travel policy: taxis and ride-hailing — Standard taxi allowance | version: 3 · status: current · effective: 2026-03-01 · supersedes: HB-47
A single taxi or ride-hailing trip on business is reimbursed up to 42 EUR. Questions about the taxi allowance go to the travel office, who keeps the record of decisions. Managers confirm claims for the taxi allowance before payment in the next payroll run.
--- HB-38 §3 | Travel policy: taxis and ride-hailing — Additions | version: 3 · status: current · effective: 2026-03-01 · supersedes: HB-47
For rides with luggage for a trade fair, 23 EUR per ride is added to the standard taxi allowance. Questions about the taxi allowance go to the travel office, who keeps the record of decisions. The travel office publishes worked examples of the taxi allowance on the intranet.
--- HB-01 §1 | Memo: taxi allowance exceptions — Purpose | version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-02-01
This memo lists group exceptions to the taxi allowance in HB-38. Managers confirm claims for the taxi allowance before payment in the next payroll run. Claims for the taxi allowance are filed in the expense tool under 'Travel policy: taxis and ride-hailing'.
--- HB-38 §1 | Travel policy: taxis and ride-hailing — Scope | version: 3 · status: current · effective: 2026-03-01 · supersedes: HB-47
This policy sets the taxi allowance for all employees. The travel office publishes worked examples of the taxi allowance on the intranet. Each claim for the taxi allowance needs a receipt showing the date, the amount and the supplier.
