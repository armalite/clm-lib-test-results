--- HB-32 §3 | Benefits policy: bike leasing — Additions | version: 3 · status: current · effective: 2026-03-01 · supersedes: HB-69
For e-bikes with an insurance package, 25 EUR per month is added to the standard bike-leasing allowance. Each claim for the bike-leasing allowance needs a receipt showing the date, the amount and the supplier. Claims for the bike-leasing allowance are filed in the expense tool under 'Benefits policy: bike leasing'.
--- HB-32 §2 | Benefits policy: bike leasing — Standard bike-leasing allowance | version: 3 · status: current · effective: 2026-03-01 · supersedes: HB-69
Employees who lease a bike for commuting receive a bike-leasing allowance of 40 EUR per month. Questions about the bike-leasing allowance go to the facilities, who keeps the record of decisions. Each claim for the bike-leasing allowance needs a receipt showing the date, the amount and the supplier.
--- HB-69 §2 | Benefits policy: bike leasing — Standard bike-leasing allowance | version: 2 · status: superseded · effective: 2025-03-01 to the day before 2026-03-01 · superseded by: HB-32
Employees who lease a bike for commuting receive a bike-leasing allowance of 70 EUR per month. Each claim for the bike-leasing allowance needs a receipt showing the date, the amount and the supplier. Managers confirm claims for the bike-leasing allowance before payment in the next payroll run.
--- HB-69 §1 | Benefits policy: bike leasing — Scope | version: 2 · status: superseded · effective: 2025-03-01 to the day before 2026-03-01 · superseded by: HB-32
This version set the bike-leasing allowance for all employees. Questions about the bike-leasing allowance go to the facilities, who keeps the record of decisions. The facilities publishes worked examples of the bike-leasing allowance on the intranet.
--- HB-32 §1 | Benefits policy: bike leasing — Scope | version: 3 · status: current · effective: 2026-03-01 · supersedes: HB-69
This policy sets the bike-leasing allowance for all employees. The facilities reviews the bike-leasing allowance once a year and announces changes in advance. Questions about the bike-leasing allowance go to the facilities, who keeps the record of decisions.
--- HB-32 §4 | Benefits policy: bike leasing — Exceptions | version: 3 · status: current · effective: 2026-03-01 · supersedes: HB-69
Group exceptions to the bike-leasing allowance are published in HB-26. The facilities publishes worked examples of the bike-leasing allowance on the intranet. Managers confirm claims for the bike-leasing allowance before payment in the next payroll run.
