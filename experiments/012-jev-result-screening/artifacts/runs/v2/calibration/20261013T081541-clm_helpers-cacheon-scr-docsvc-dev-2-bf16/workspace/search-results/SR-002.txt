--- HB-16 §2 | Remote work: home-office equipment — Standard home-office equipment allowance | version: 2 · status: superseded · effective: 2025-03-01 to the day before 2026-04-01 · superseded by: HB-63
Employees may claim a one-off home-office equipment allowance of 350 EUR. The facilities publishes worked examples of the home-office equipment allowance on the intranet. Each claim for the home-office equipment allowance needs a receipt showing the date, the amount and the supplier.
--- HB-63 §4 | Remote work: home-office equipment — Exceptions | version: 3 · status: current · effective: 2026-04-01 · supersedes: HB-16
Group exceptions to the home-office equipment allowance are published in HB-46. Claims for the home-office equipment allowance are filed in the expense tool under 'Remote work: home-office equipment'. Managers confirm claims for the home-office equipment allowance before payment in the next payroll run.
--- HB-63 §2 | Remote work: home-office equipment — Standard home-office equipment allowance | version: 3 · status: current · effective: 2026-04-01 · supersedes: HB-16
Employees may claim a one-off home-office equipment allowance of 400 EUR. The facilities reviews the home-office equipment allowance once a year and announces changes in advance. Each claim for the home-office equipment allowance needs a receipt showing the date, the amount and the supplier.
--- HB-46 §1 | Memo: home-office equipment allowance exceptions — Purpose | version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-03-01
This memo lists group exceptions to the home-office equipment allowance in HB-63. The facilities publishes worked examples of the home-office equipment allowance on the intranet. Questions about the home-office equipment allowance go to the facilities, who keeps the record of decisions.
--- HB-39 §1 | Related budgets: home-office equipment — Scope | version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-03-01
This document covers budgets related to, but separate from, the home-office equipment allowance. Managers confirm claims for the home-office equipment allowance before payment in the next payroll run. The facilities publishes worked examples of the home-office equipment allowance on the intranet.
