--- HB-53 §2 | Memo: nightly lodging limit exceptions — Exception for business trips to Zurich, Geneva or Oslo | version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-05-01
For business trips to Zurich, Geneva or Oslo, the nightly lodging limit is 250 EUR per night instead of the standard amount. The travel office reviews the nightly lodging limit once a year and announces changes in advance. Managers confirm claims for the nightly lodging limit before payment in the next payroll run.
--- HB-35 §2 | Travel policy: lodging — Standard nightly lodging limit | version: 2 · status: superseded · effective: 2025-05-01 to the day before 2026-02-01 · superseded by: HB-64
The nightly lodging limit for business travel was 205 EUR per night, including taxes and city fees. The travel office publishes worked examples of the nightly lodging limit on the intranet. Claims for the nightly lodging limit are filed in the expense tool under 'Travel policy: lodging'.
--- HB-64 §2 | Travel policy: lodging — Standard nightly lodging limit | version: 3 · status: current · effective: 2026-02-01 · supersedes: HB-35
The nightly lodging limit for business travel is 210 EUR per night, including taxes and city fees. Questions about the nightly lodging limit go to the travel office, who keeps the record of decisions. The travel office publishes worked examples of the nightly lodging limit on the intranet.
