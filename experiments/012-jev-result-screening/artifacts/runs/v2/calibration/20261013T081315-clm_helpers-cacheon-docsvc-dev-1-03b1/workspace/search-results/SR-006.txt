--- HB-11 §2 | Facilities policy: parking — Standard monthly parking allowance | version: 2 · status: superseded · effective: 2025-09-01 to the day before 2026-04-01 · superseded by: HB-23
Employees who drive to the office receive a monthly parking allowance of 90 EUR. The facilities publishes worked examples of the monthly parking allowance on the intranet. The facilities reviews the monthly parking allowance once a year and announces changes in advance.
--- HB-23 §2 | Facilities policy: parking — Standard monthly parking allowance | version: 3 · status: current · effective: 2026-04-01 · supersedes: HB-11
Employees who drive to the office receive a monthly parking allowance of 60 EUR. Claims for the monthly parking allowance are filed in the expense tool under 'Facilities policy: parking'. The facilities reviews the monthly parking allowance once a year and announces changes in advance.
--- HB-46 §2 | Memo: monthly parking allowance exceptions — Exception for employees at the city-centre office | version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-09-01
For employees at the city-centre office, the monthly parking allowance is 110 EUR per month instead of the standard amount. The facilities reviews the monthly parking allowance once a year and announces changes in advance. Each claim for the monthly parking allowance needs a receipt showing the date, the amount and the supplier.
--- HB-46 §1 | Memo: monthly parking allowance exceptions — Purpose | version: 1 · status: current · effective: 2025-09-01
This memo lists group exceptions to the monthly parking allowance in HB-23. Questions about the monthly parking allowance go to the facilities, who keeps the record of decisions. Managers confirm claims for the monthly parking allowance before payment in the next payroll run.
--- HB-23 §1 | Facilities policy: parking — Scope | version: 3 · status: current · effective: 2026-04-01 · supersedes: HB-11
This policy sets the monthly parking allowance for all employees. The facilities reviews the monthly parking allowance once a year and announces changes in advance. The facilities publishes worked examples of the monthly parking allowance on the intranet.

Group exceptions to the monthly parking allowance are published in HB-46. Each claim for the monthly parking allowance needs a receipt showing the date, the amount and the supplier. The facilities publishes worked examples of the monthly parking allowance on the intranet.
